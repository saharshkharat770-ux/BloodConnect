Knowledge Base

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions on voluntary donation, cold-chain safety, and emergency hospital integration.

How quickly can blood units be dispatched in an emergency trauma case?
Under Level 1 Trauma Protocol, BloodConnect cross-matches nearest verified inventory within 3 minutes and initiates cold-chain transit with an average delivery window of 18 minutes across affiliated metropolitan trauma centers.
Who is eligible to register as a voluntary blood donor?
Any healthy individual aged 18 to 65 weighing at least 50 kg with normal hemoglobin (minimum 12.5 g/dL) and who has completed the mandatory 90-day cooldown interval since their previous donation may donate.
How does BloodConnect monitor cold-chain storage safety?
All regional vault chambers and mobile transit refrigerators maintain continuous digital temperature telemetry strictly calibrated between +2°C and +6°C for whole blood and red cells, and -18°C for fresh frozen plasma.
How can a new medical facility or hospital join the network?
Accredited hospitals can apply using the Institutional Inquiry form above. Once license and cold-storage compliance are verified by our clinical committee, your facility is provisioned with digital requisition access.
